The Axiom

An AI data-driven tool for fleet management. It does not only collect fleet data. It explains it.

Fleet operators lose money in places they cannot see: fuel that goes missing, trucks running inefficiently, trips that do not add up. The Axiom reads daily fleet records, spots what is abnormal, and tells managers what to do about it.

DataFuel, odometer and trip records captured per truck.
AnalysisKm per litre compared across trucks and over time.
RiskStatistical checks flag suspicious and fraudulent entries.
InsightAn AI assistant, Ottilia, explains what the numbers mean.
ActionManagers get clear alerts and exportable reports.

Fuel efficiency tracking

Log odometer readings and diesel added, then compare each truck's efficiency against a baseline.

Anomaly and fraud alerts

Absolute thresholds, z-scores and moving-average deviation catch unusual fuel use. Alerts can be filtered by fraud or suspicious.

Trip logging

Record departures and returns. Distance and time on the road are calculated automatically.

Ottilia, the AI assistant

Ask questions about your fleet in plain language and get answers grounded in your own data.

Companies and departments

Admins oversee every department in a company. Each department keeps its own trucks and records.

Built for real operations

Multiple currencies, English and Chinese, dark and light themes, CSV export and data backup. Data stays local.
